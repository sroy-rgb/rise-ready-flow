Colorado justices rule tenants have right to a jury trial in eviction cases

In a landmark 4-2 decision, the Colorado Supreme Court ruled that tenants have the right to a jury trial in eviction cases — a victory CEDP and CED Law attorneys fought for through appellate litigation.

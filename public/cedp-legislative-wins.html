HB24-1098

For Cause Eviction of a Tenant

Signed by Governor Jared Polis · April 2024

Governor Jared Polis signed House Majority Leader Monica Duran, Rep. Javier Mabrey, and Sens. Julie Gonzales and Nick Hinrichsen's For Cause Eviction legislation into law in April 2024. This legislation protects thousands of Colorado tenants who face unjust evictions and helps stabilize more households in their communities.

What this law does:

  • Protects against racial and gender discrimination in evictions
  • Prevents households from unnecessarily experiencing displacement and homelessness
  • Reduces the number of evictions in Colorado and protects against gentrification
  • Creates long-term stability for both tenants and landlords
  • Saves Colorado families money by keeping a roof over their heads
Sponsors: House Majority Leader Monica Duran, Rep. Javier Mabrey, Sens. Julie Gonzales and Nick Hinrichsen

SB24-094

Safe Housing for Residential Tenants

Signed into law · May 2024 · Effective immediately

Sponsored by Sens. Julie Gonzales and Tony Exum, along with Reps. Mandy Lindsay and Meg Froelich, SB24-094 ensures Colorado tenants can more easily use the state's Warranty of Habitability Law to address health and safety hazards in their housing.

What this law does:

  • Sets reasonable notice requirements so tenants don't need an attorney to ask for repairs
  • Sets presumptive timeframes for completing repairs with flexibility for landlords
  • Clarifies when landlords must provide alternative lodging during repairs
  • Establishes policies allowing tenants to use appropriate cooling in extreme heat
  • Outlines appropriate legal remedies if a rental becomes uninhabitable
Sponsors: Sens. Julie Gonzales and Tony Exum; Reps. Mandy Lindsay and Meg Froelich

HB24-1051

Towing Carrier Regulation

Signed into law · May 2024

Addresses a growing problem CEDP encountered serving clients: Colorado's towing system needs reform. Investigations by the Colorado Attorney General's office found that one of the biggest towing companies in the state violated numerous regulations, including the Towing Bill of Rights — a CEDP priority bill passed in 2022.

What this law does:

  • Gives the Public Utilities Commission (PUC) authority to revoke towing carrier licenses for specific violations
  • Prohibits for-profit towing companies from patrolling residential lots
  • Requires landlords and property managers to direct tows in writing via a signed form
  • Creates additional enforcement mechanisms for District Attorneys and the Colorado AG
Sponsors: Reps. Andrew Boesenecker and Tisha Mauro; Sens. Julie Gonzalez and Kevin Priola

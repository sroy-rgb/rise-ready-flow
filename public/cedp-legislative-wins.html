Towing Bill of Rights

After hundreds of CEDP clients lost their cars to predatory towing, we organized impacted Coloradans, gathered data, and drafted HB22-1314 with legislators. It is now the strongest towing consumer protection law in the country.

12,000+vehicles protected annually

Wyatt's Towing Settlement

CEDP partnered with the Colorado Attorney General to bring a landmark case against the state's largest towing operator. The $1M settlement returned money directly to families who had lost cars they could not afford to recover.

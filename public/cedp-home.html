Who we are

Born from a Facebook post. Built into Colorado's economic defense.

In April 2020, two lawyers asked on Facebook who needed help with eviction. Thousands responded. What started as a volunteer effort has grown into a team of 200 serving 68,000 people across 59 counties.

We run a financial emergency room — integrating legal defense, financial aid, and resource navigation in a single point of contact.

When someone comes to us in crisis, we don't send them to five different offices. We meet them where they are.Sam Gilman — Co-Founder & Co-CEO

Legislative wins

+
2020

Federal Eviction Moratorium

CEDP research underpinned the federal moratorium and the $46 billion national investment in emergency rental assistance

2022

Towing Bill of Rights

First statewide towing consumer protection law in Colorado

2023

Expanded Renters' Rights

Strengthened eviction protections including longer notice periods and right to cure

Towing Reform & Fair Housing

Banned automatic tows, established Fair Housing Unit. Wyatts Towing settled for $1M.

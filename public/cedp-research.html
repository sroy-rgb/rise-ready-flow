Featured Investigation

Inside Colorado's Private Property Towing Industry

Towing in Colorado is out of control. Over the past two years, our clients have had their vehicles repeatedly towed from the apartment complexes and mobile home parks where they live, often without warning or justification. This investigation led directly to the Towing Bill of Rights and HB24-1051.

Featured Research

The Hidden Link Between Medical Debt & Eviction

Zach Neumann, Sam Gilman, Priya Patel • June 2024

A two-year study of 24,000 Colorado eviction filings finds that 41% of households facing eviction had unpaid medical debt in the prior 18 months — and that medical debt was the strongest single predictor of housing loss. This report outlines policy interventions tested in three Colorado counties.
